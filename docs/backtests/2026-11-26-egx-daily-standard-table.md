# ICT engine, EGX Daily — standard backtest table (2026-11-26)

> **Test**: offline port `tools/backtest/ict-engine.mjs` (matches TradingView trade-for-trade on dividend-adjusted bars), current = Preset EGX Daily, pullback + trailing-stop models (what the scanner and dashboard trade). 101 EGX30/EGX70 stocks, TradingView daily, dividend-adjusted, 2001-05-15 to 2026-11-25. Hold-out = entries from 2025-11-16.

> Each trade in % of entry: the gross move blended over the TP1/TP2/TP3 thirds, minus 0.15% commission per side (no slippage), as in the Smart Confluence standard. Total Win / Total Loss = sum of winning / losing trades (%), Net = their sum, PF = win ÷ loss. Losing Streak and Max Drawdown: all stocks together in entry order, summed trade % (not compounded). Before → after: 🟢 better / 🔴 worse (smaller loss, shorter streak, shallower drawdown are better; Total Trades has no mark).

> **Data clean-up**: the feed leaves some share restructurings unadjusted (67 one-day moves beyond x1.45 / x0.6 in 44 stocks). TradingView shows the same bars, but a trade held across one is not real, so those trades are left out of every row (current: 6 trades, +118% net).
